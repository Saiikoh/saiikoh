About Saiikoh Studios

02

One developer.
One studio.
A lot of ideas.

Saiikoh Studios is an indie digital studio primarily representing Saiikoh and his work. It serves as the home for projects, experiments, products and ideas created around AI, web development, software and emerging technology.

The studio exists to turn concepts into real, usable digital experiences from AI platforms and websites to experimental software that may start as nothing more than an idea.

Saiikoh Studios is intentionally indie. There is no large team hiding behind the name. It represents the work, vision, experimentation and development carried out under the Saiikoh identity.

Some projects become products. Some remain experiments. Some simply exist because they were interesting enough to build.

The common goal is simple: build something worth using.
